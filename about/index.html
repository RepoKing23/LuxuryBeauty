Meet Cleo R.

A journey rooted in passion, precision, and patient care.

It didn’t begin in a spa.
It began in nursing.

In 2013, Cleo Rukovo started her career as a Registered Nurse, dedicating herself to patient care and developing a deep appreciation for the complexity of the human body.

Over time, that clinical foundation grew into a broader fascination with how health, confidence, and appearance intersect.

In 2022, she became a Nurse Practitioner — an advanced foundation from which to approach aesthetic medicine.

Medical knowledge, aesthetic artistry, thoughtful assessment, and human connection.

A NP first. An aesthetic practitioner with intention.

Cleo’s advanced clinical background influences every aspect of her approach. Her role is to assess, educate, guide, treat appropriately, and provide thoughtful care throughout the experience.

  • Medical history
  • Facial anatomy
  • Muscle movement
  • Skin health
  • Individual goals
  • Treatment suitability

The Cleo difference

More than aesthetics. A relationship built on trust.

What makes Luxury Beauty by Cleo R. different isn’t simply what happens during a treatment. It’s the approach behind it: assess first, recommend honestly, and treat with restraint.

“I don’t just inject.
I assess.”— Cleo R.

Clinical expertise

Cleo’s foundation began in nursing and advanced into Nurse Practitioner practice. That clinical background shapes how she assesses, treats, monitors care, and thinks about safety.

Artistic eye

Aesthetic care is also about balance, proportion, movement, and understanding how individual features work together.

Natural results

The goal is for you to look refreshed, confident, and like yourself — enhancement rather than alteration.

Honest recommendations

If a treatment isn’t appropriate, Cleo will say so. Sometimes the best treatment is no treatment at all.

Genuine connection

Cleo listens to people’s stories and believes the relationship between practitioner and client is part of the experience itself.

Inclusive care

There is no universal template for beauty.

As a Black woman and woman of color, Cleo understands the importance of representation and individualized care in aesthetics.

People have different skin tones, facial structures, features, and aesthetic goals. Luxury Beauty by Cleo R. is committed to creating an environment where people of all backgrounds feel welcomed, understood, respected, and represented.

Particular attention is given to understanding the needs and nuances that can come with melanin-rich skin and diverse facial structures, including how your skin heals and responds to treatment.
