Why it happens

Knowing the cause helps Cleo choose the right option. Most people have more than one of these at once.

01

Movement

Lines form where muscles crease the skin over and over: the forehead, between the brows and around the eyes.

02

Collagen loss

From your mid-20s, skin makes less collagen each year, so creases stay after your face relaxes.

03

Sun exposure

UV light breaks down collagen and elastin. It’s the biggest outside cause of early lines.

04

Dehydration

Dry skin shows fine, crepey lines more, especially under the eyes and on the neck.

What to expect

  • Movement lines soften over about 2 weeks.
  • Deep, etched lines improve, but may need a few visits or a combination of options.
  • Treatment can’t stop ageing. Regular care slows how deep lines set in.

Care at home

  • ✓Wear SPF 30 or higher every day, including winter
  • ✓Ask Cleo whether a nightly retinoid suits your skin
  • ✓Moisturize morning and night
  • ✓Avoid smoking, which speeds up collagen loss
