Why it happens

Knowing the cause helps Cleo choose the right option. Most people have more than one of these at once.

01

Hormones and metabolism

Hormones affect hunger, fullness and how your body stores fat.

02

Medications

Some medications change appetite or make weight harder to lose.

03

Sleep and stress

Short sleep and long-term stress both raise appetite and cravings.

04

Life stages

Menopause, recovery after pregnancy and ageing all change how your body responds.
