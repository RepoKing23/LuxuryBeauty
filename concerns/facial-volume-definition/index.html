Why it happens

Knowing the cause helps Cleo choose the right option. Most people have more than one of these at once.

01

Fat pads shift

The fat pads in your cheeks shrink and move lower with age, which flattens the mid-face.

02

Bone changes

Facial bones slowly lose density, so the skin above has less support.

03

Weight loss

Losing weight, including on a medical program, often shows first in the face.

04

Genetics

Some people are born with flatter cheekbones or a less defined chin.

What to expect

  • You’ll see filler results right away. They settle at about 2 weeks.
  • Cleo restores volume in stages, so your face stays in balance.
  • Filler can’t lift heavy, loose skin. If that’s your main concern, Cleo will tell you.

Care at home
