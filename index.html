Every visit.

Aesthetic care should never feel like pressure. Here’s what happens behind every treatment, so you know what to expect.

01

Health screening

Cleo reviews your medical history, medications and allergies before any treatment.

02

Informed consent

You’ll know the plan, the risks and the aftercare, and sign consent before anything starts.

03

Sterile, single-use supplies

Every needle and cannula is new, sealed and used once.

04

A plan for the unexpected

Cleo keeps what she needs to manage a reaction or reverse filler on site.

05

Honest advice

If a treatment isn’t right for you, she’ll tell you. Sometimes the answer is “not today.”
