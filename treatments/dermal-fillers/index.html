What are dermal fillers?

Dermal fillers are hyaluronic acid gels. Your body already makes hyaluronic acid, and the gel holds water to add structure where your face has lost volume or definition.

Cleo uses firmer gels for structure (cheeks, chin, jaw) and softer ones where movement matters. Hyaluronic acid filler is temporary and can be dissolved with an enzyme if needed. Filler can add shape and support. It can’t tighten loose skin or replace surgery.

Results, recovery and aftercare

Results

You’ll see the change right away. Swelling settles over 1–2 weeks, and the final shape shows at about 2 weeks. Filler typically lasts 9 to 18 months, longer in firmer areas like the chin and jaw.

Side effects

Swelling, tenderness and bruising are common for a few days. Small firm spots usually settle by themselves. Rare risks include infection and filler blocking a blood vessel. Call the clinic right away if you notice severe pain or skin that turns pale, blue or blotchy.

Aftercare

  • Skip hard exercise and alcohol for 24 hours
  • Avoid saunas and hot yoga for 48 hours
  • Don’t press or massage the area
  • Wait 2 weeks before dental work
