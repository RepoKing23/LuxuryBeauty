What are Botox and neuromodulators?

Neuromodulators are purified proteins that relax the specific muscles that crease the skin when you frown, squint or raise your brows. When the muscle moves less, the line above it softens.

Cleo uses small, measured doses placed where your muscles pull hardest. The aim is a rested look. You should still be able to frown, smile and raise your brows. Results wear off as the muscle recovers, so nothing is permanent.

Results, recovery and aftercare

Results

You’ll notice a softening from day 3–5. The full effect shows at 2 weeks and typically lasts 3–4 months. Many clients find results last longer after a few regular treatments.

Side effects

Small bumps at the injection points fade within 20 minutes. Pinpoint bruising and a mild headache are common and short-lived. A temporary heavy brow or eyelid is rare and settles as the product wears off.

Aftercare

  • Stay upright for 4 hours
  • Don’t rub or massage the area that day
  • Skip hard exercise until the next day
  • Avoid facials and saunas for 24 hours
