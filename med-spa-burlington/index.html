Getting here

From Burlington to our Oakville clinic.

From central Burlington

Take the QEW or Highway 403 east toward Oakville, then head north to Dundas Street. Most trips take about 20–25 minutes, depending on traffic.

From north Burlington

Dundas Street (Highway 5) runs east from Burlington straight toward the clinic, a direct route for Alton Village, Millcroft, Headon Forest and Tyandaga.
